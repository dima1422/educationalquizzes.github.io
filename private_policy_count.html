Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Count Things - Any Objects Counter (the "App"), a mobile/web application that uses computer vision and AI technology to count objects in user-uploaded pictures.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Images and photos you upload for object counting
  • Account information (if you create an account): email address, username, password
  • Feedback, support requests, or communications with us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (how you interact with the App, features used, session duration)
  • IP address and approximate location (country level)
  • Analytics data via third-party tools

2.3 Image Data

Images you upload are processed to detect and count objects. Depending on your settings, these images may be temporarily stored on our servers during processing.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core service: analyze images and return object counts
  • To improve our AI models (we may use anonymized or aggregated data for training)
  • To personalize your experience
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal data or images to third parties.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who assist in operating the App (cloud hosting, AI processing, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Images are automatically deleted after processing unless you explicitly choose to save them. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Opt-out of certain data processing (where applicable)
  • Request that we stop processing your images for model training
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child, we will take steps to delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.
